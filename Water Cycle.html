💧 The Water Cycle

Follow water as it moves through Earth’s atmosphere and surface.

♨️ EvaporationSunlight heats liquid water into water vapor.
☁️ CondensationCooling vapor forms tiny droplets and clouds.
🌧️ PrecipitationWater falls as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
🌊 CollectionWater gathers in rivers, lakes, oceans, and soil.
1. EvaporationLiquid → gas
2. CondensationGas → droplets
3. PrecipitationClouds release water
4. CollectionWater returns and gathers
